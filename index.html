FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my friends need to install the app?

No. They open the link you share and tap their items in the browser. Only the person who scans the bill needs to start it.

How are tax and service charge split?

In proportion to what each person ordered, in the same order the receipt applies them. Someone who only had a drink doesn’t subsidize a full meal.

What if the scan gets an item wrong?

Doubtful lines are flagged, and you can fix a name or price, add a missing item or remove one in a tap before sharing.

How does payment work?

Each person gets their exact share and pays through their UPI app. Paid shares show up for the whole group.

Can we settle at the end of a trip instead?

Yes. Add each bill to the trip tab, see running balances, and settle everything with a few payments at the end.
